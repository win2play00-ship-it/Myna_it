Delete Account

Information about deleting your Myna account and associated data.

Delete Your Myna Account

Myna allows users to permanently delete their account directly from within the mobile application.

To delete your account:

  1. Open the Myna app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Requirements Before Deletion

Before your account can be deleted, the following conditions must be met:

  • Your wallet balance must be ₹0.
  • Any pending withdrawals should be completed.
  • Your account must not be under fraud investigation or legal review.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your account is deleted, the following personal information will be permanently removed or anonymized where applicable:

  • Mobile Number
  • Password
  • Refresh Tokens
  • Notification Data
  • Saved Payout Destinations

Your account will no longer be accessible after deletion.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain records may be retained where required by applicable laws, financial regulations, fraud prevention requirements, dispute resolution, or security purposes.

These records may include:

  • Deposit Records
  • Withdrawal Records
  • Wallet Ledger Entries
  • Payment Transactions
  • Quiz Match History
  • Financial Audit Logs

Where possible, retained records are no longer associated with your active account and are preserved only for legal, accounting, security, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are generally processed immediately after all eligibility conditions are satisfied.
