1. Our Commitment

At Myna, we are committed to providing a safe, fair, and enjoyable gaming experience. We encourage all users to participate responsibly and within their personal financial limits. Real-money gaming should always be considered a form of entertainment and not a source of income.

2. Play Responsibly

Before participating in any contest, you should:

  • Play only for entertainment.
  • Use only money you can afford to lose.
  • Set personal spending limits.
  • Take regular breaks while playing.
  • Avoid continuous gameplay for extended periods.
  • Maintain a healthy balance between gaming and daily life.

3. Do Not Chase Losses

If you lose a contest, do not immediately attempt to recover your losses by playing additional matches. Every quiz contest is independent, and previous outcomes do not influence future results.

4. Age Restriction

Myna is intended only for users who are legally permitted to participate in real-money skill-based gaming under applicable laws. Users below the legally permitted age must not create an account or use the platform. If we discover that an underage user has registered, the account may be suspended or permanently terminated.

5. Self-Control Tips

To enjoy gaming responsibly, we recommend that users:

  • Set a monthly entertainment budget.
  • Track deposits and withdrawals.
  • Avoid borrowing money for gaming.
  • Never play while emotionally distressed.
  • Never play under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Stop playing if gaming is negatively affecting your personal or professional life.

6. Warning Signs

Gaming may become unhealthy if you experience any of the following:

  • Playing beyond your financial limits.
  • Ignoring work, studies, or family responsibilities.
  • Trying to recover previous losses.
  • Feeling stressed or anxious about gaming.
  • Borrowing money to continue playing.
  • Hiding your gaming activity from others.

If you recognize these signs, we encourage you to stop playing and seek support.

7. Account Controls

To promote responsible gaming, Myna may implement features such as:

  • Account verification.
  • KYC verification.
  • Withdrawal verification.
  • Fraud monitoring.
  • Account suspension for suspicious activity.
  • Temporary restrictions where required for user safety.

8. Fair Gaming Environment

Myna uses secure server-side systems to ensure:

  • Fair matchmaking.
  • Accurate score calculation.
  • Equal opportunity for all players.
  • Secure wallet processing.
  • Transparent prize distribution.

9. User Responsibility

Each user is responsible for:

  • Keeping account credentials secure.
  • Monitoring personal spending.
  • Using the platform responsibly.
  • Complying with all applicable laws.
  • Providing accurate account information.

10. Suspension of Accounts

Myna reserves the right to temporarily or permanently suspend accounts that:

  • Violate platform policies.
  • Engage in fraudulent activity.
  • Use multiple accounts.
  • Attempt to manipulate contests.
  • Pose security or financial risks.

11. Support

If you believe your gaming habits are becoming unhealthy, we encourage you to stop playing and seek assistance from trusted family members, friends, or professional counseling services available in your region.

12. Changes to this Policy

Myna may update this Responsible Gaming Policy from time to time. Any changes become effective immediately after publication on this page. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
